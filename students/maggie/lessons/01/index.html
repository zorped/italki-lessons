1.2

Make your ideas sound more natural

Learn the complete sentence patterns, then change the details to fit your own situation.

I spread myself too thin across too many activities.

Use spread myself too thin when your attention is divided.

I'm still not very good at technical skills or English.

Use be good at followed by a noun or an -ing form.

I want to spend less time on other activities.

Use spend time on an activity.

I want to devote most of my time to improving my English and developing my technical skills.

After devote time to, use a noun or an -ing form.

Everyone has the same twenty-four hours each day.

Use the same, and remember the verb has after everyone.

Direction is the prerequisite, and effort is the amplifier. Effort without direction is merely depletion. Only directed obsessive effort has the power to change your life. Therefore, you do not need to go all-in on everything. You only need to commit fully to that one thing you have confirmed.

Phrase and ideaTest before committing fullyTry a direction on a small scale, examine real feedback, and invest more only when the evidence supports it.

This sequence — confirm first, then commit fully — is far more stable than rushing into total dedication at once. You are not investing irrationally. You are rationally and deliberately pushing yourself toward a higher level.

Page 8

There is another psychological reason why obsessive effort can transform your life. When you invest far more effort than most people into one pursuit, you develop a deep sense of identity with it. You are no longer merely "doing this thing"; you become part of it. This identity changes how you face setbacks.

You will not give up easily, because you are not just completing a task — you are shaping yourself. Every hardship you overcome becomes part of who you are.

Those who transform their lives are rarely the most gifted. They are the ones who devote themselves to one thing far more deeply than those around them.

Page 9

They do not win by defeating others; they win by going further. And that extra distance eventually changes their standing in this field.

Obsessive effort can truly change your life. This does not mean effort guarantees success. It means sufficient effort can shift you to a new space. There, the rules are different, opportunities are different, and what you are capable of becomes different.

You do not need to envy those who have rewritten their lives. You can choose to become one of them. Not everyone needs this level of obsession. You only need to be willing to bet everything on that one thing.

Page 10

True life transformation never comes from waiting for fate to hand you a chance. It comes from pushing past the ordinary in one area, until a new version of yourself is created.
