Hotel role-play

First attempt the conversation directly from the book. Then use the language support below to make your second attempt smoother.

9.1

Your conversation goals

You have just arrived at a hotel. Speak to the receptionist without writing a full script first.

Request the right roomExplain how long you will stayMention visitorsAsk about mealsAsk walking timeAsk about a phone callMention onward travelAsk about dining with guests
Open language supportAfter your first attempt

हमें दो लोगों के लिए एक कमरा चाहिए।

hamen do logon ke lie ek kamrā cāhie.

We need one room for two people.

हम दो दिन रहेंगे।

ham do din rahenge.

We will stay for two days.

आज शाम कुछ दोस्त हमसे मिलने आएँगे।

āj śām kuch dost hamse milne āenge.

Some friends will come to meet us this evening.

नाश्ते में क्या मिलेगा?

nāśte men kyā milegā?

What will be available for breakfast?

क्या रात का खाना भी मिलेगा?

kyā rāt kā khānā bhī milegā?

Will dinner be available too?

वहाँ तक पैदल जाने में कितना समय लगेगा?

vahān tak paidal jāne men kitnā samay lagegā?

How long will it take to walk there?

क्या मैं कमरे से लंदन फ़ोन कर सकती हूँ?

kyā main kamre se London fon kar saktī hūn?

Can I phone London from the room?

क्या मेरे दोस्त आज रात मेरे साथ यहाँ खाना खा सकते हैं?

kyā mere dost āj rāt mere sāth yahān khānā khā sakte hain?

Can my friends eat here with me tonight?
